# Grasp All Lose All

Once there lived a poor farmer in a certain village. He supported his family with great hardship. One day, he bought a goose. It was a magical goose because it used to lay a golden egg daily. He used to sell the golden egg at the market. He became rich.

But by this time, the man became very greedy and impatient to have all the eggs at a time. He thought that if he cut the goose, he would get all the eggs at a time. And in this way, he would be richer overnight.

So, he told his wife his plan. His wife was a wise woman, and she was not greedy. So she requested her husband not to do so. But the farmer did not listen to his wife. Finally, he cut its stomach with a sharp knife.

But alas! There was no egg in it. The farmer was sorry. His wife also scolded him. She said, “You are foolish but greedy. So you have lost all.”
