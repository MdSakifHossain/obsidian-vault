# A Fox Without a Tail

Once there lived a very clever fox in a jungle. One day, while walking through the jungle, he fell into a trap and lost his tail.

Without a tail, he looked very strange, and he felt ashamed. He also felt very unhappy and sad.

But the fox was clever and took shelter in a cave for a few days. Then he came out of the cave and called all the foxes of the forest to a meeting.

He said, “Friends! God has given us our tails, but they are a useless burden to us.

So, I have cut off my tail, and I hope all of you will follow me.” An old fox knew about the incident. He revealed this fact in the meeting. Then and there, the tailless fox ran away into the forest.
